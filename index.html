Behind every perfectly folded garment is a team of over 80 smiling, passionate professionals who treat your clothes like their own. We don't just do laundry — we deliver confidence, comfort, and care right to your door.

Our state-of-the-art facility runs industrial-grade, modern washing machines engineered to handle everything from everyday casuals to the most delicate silk — all without compromising the fabric's integrity or colour.

We combine advanced textile science with a human touch — precision, warmth, and zero shortcuts. That's the Washington Laundry way.

Whether it's a single shirt or an entire wardrobe refresh, we're ready to get it done — with a smile, every single time.
